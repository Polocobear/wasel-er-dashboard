Waiting for the WASEL dashboard

This screen mirrors the dashboard. It runs no simulation of its own, so it stays empty until the dashboard is open.

  1. Open the WASEL dashboard (index.html) in another tab of this browser.
  2. Stock, supply requests and blood standby appear here within 2 seconds.
  3. Approve orders by tapping who you are, then Approve. There is nothing to type.
