WASEL · Architecture & data map

ER Crisis Command Center for Nexus 26, Track 3 (Informatics). Ten hospital systems and the paramedic responder app feed one FHIR R4 gateway. An event engine and AI decision layer turn that data into suggestions, and each screen shows only what its role needs. AI does the watching, counting and matching. Humans do the judging, treating and deciding.

10 source systems 1 responder pre-alert feed 5 role-filtered screens HL7 FHIR R4 · HL7 v2.5 · IEEE 11073 · LOINC · ICD-10-CM All patient data is fictional

Patient journey

One patient, end to end, and the system that feeds each step.

  1. Step 1

    Detection

    EMS · CAD

    998/997 call, smart traffic cameras or a building fire-alarm link opens an incident. Mode can step up to MCI.

  2. Step 2

    Dispatch

    EMS · CAD

    Ambulances on scene, field triage (ESI, or START colours in MCI), live ETAs.

  3. Step 3

    Scene / ID

    Responder app + Face-ID

    Face match returns a candidate only. With no match, care goes on under a temporary T-###.

  4. Step 4

    Pre-alert

    Pre-alert v1 → gateway

    ATMIST and safety summary (Riayati, Malaffi, NABIDH). AI proposes a bay and pages the team.

  5. Step 5

    Arrival

    ADT A04 · FHIR Location

    Registered; a reserved bed means door-to-bed 0 min. A nurse confirms identity at the door.

  6. Step 6

    Treatment

    IEEE 11073 · LIS · RIS · Pharmacy · RTLS · Roster

    NEWS2 watch, CT queue, blood and supplies, nurse load and hours.

  7. Step 7

    Disposition

    ADT A03 / A02 · EHR

    Home, ward, ICU or OR. The family board shows status only, never medical details.

Human in the loop

The AI only suggests. Each suggestion names who may approve it, explains why, and is written to the audit log when accepted or dismissed. Approval rights come from ROLES[…].act in src/00-core.js.

Never automated

  • Diagnosis, treatment and orders stay with clinicians.
  • Chart merging: a biometric match is only a candidate until a nurse confirms it.
  • Mode changes (Steady → Strain → Surge → MCI) follow published thresholds, but the actions they trigger, such as a silent surge, still need approval.
  • Dismissed suggestions stay suppressed for 10 min, and the dismissal is audited.
