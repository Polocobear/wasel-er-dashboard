# WASEL · Industry & Regulatory Compliance

**WASEL ER Command Dashboard** · Nexus 26, Track 3 (Informatics) · prototype with synthetic data only.
Every row below points to the file and function that does the work. Anything marked **Production design** is planned and is not in the prototype. **†** means the feature is specified in `spec-v2.md` and the engine function exists, but the UI wiring must be confirmed in the final build (see section 4).

---

## 1. Summary

WASEL brings ten hospital systems into one screen. It speaks the formats those systems already use: **HL7 FHIR R4** and **HL7 v2.5** for records and events, **IEEE 11073 SDC** for bedside monitors, **LOINC** for vital signs and labs, and **ICD-10-CM** for diagnoses, with **SNOMED CT** as the planned mapping target. Clinical decisions use validated scales: **ESI v4** in the ED, **START** in the field during a mass-casualty incident, and **NEWS2** for deterioration. The design follows **ISO 9241-210** (human-centred design) and targets **WCAG 2.2 AA**. Security uses **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** Annex A as the control catalogue. The **HIPAA Security Rule** and **NIST SP 800-66 Rev. 2** serve only as comparison frameworks, because they are US rules. For the UAE, the prototype follows **Federal Law No. 2 of 2019** (ICT in health fields), **ADHICS**, the consent rules of the **NABIDH, Malaffi and Riayati** exchanges, and the **PDPL** principles for biometric data. Emergency operations follow **JCI** patient-safety and emergency-management requirements and the **NCEMA** crisis framework. In the prototype, every AI output is a suggestion that a human approves or dismisses. Every chart view and every decision is logged. Names are masked by role, and a face match is only ever a *candidate* identity that never merges records on its own.

## 2. Standards map

### 2a. Interoperability and terminology

| Standard | Area | Where in WASEL | How it complies (justification) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HL7 FHIR R4** (4.0.1) | Interoperability API | Patient drawer "FHIR R4 Bundle" (`30-ui.js fhirBundle`); integration feed; pre-alert intake (`15-wasel.js receivePreAlert`); footer **Data map** (10 systems → standard → panel, `00-core.js SOURCES`) | The Bundle (`type: collection`) holds an **Encounter** (class `EMER`, v3-ActCode; status planned / triaged / in-progress; ESI priority), a **Condition** (system `http://hl7.org/fhir/sid/icd-10-cm`) and 5 **Observations** (category `vital-signs`, LOINC code, UCUM `valueQuantity`, device = IEEE 11073 SDC patch). **Patient** is referenced as `subject: Patient/P-1001` and is not copied, so demographics stay in the source EHR (minimum necessary). The gateway feed shows FHIR messages for **Location** (bed occupied / reserved / cleaned), **SupplyRequest** (blood and kit restocks), **MedicationRequest** (orders with WHO ATC code) and **ServiceRequest** (partner-clinic offer). These feed messages are simulated: they name the resource and its event but carry no full payload. |
| **HL7 v2.5** ADT / ORU | Event messaging | Integration feed, journey strip, patient list (`10-engine.js arrive / leave`, lab results) | `ADT^A04` registers an arrival, `ADT^A02` a transfer to ward, ICU or OR, and `ADT^A03` a discharge. `ORU^R01` delivers LOINC-coded lab results with critical-value flags. Most hospital ADT and lab systems still send v2, so the gateway accepts it and normalises it into one patient model. |
| **IEEE 11073 SDC** | Device connectivity | Monitor feed (`10-engine.js`, e.g. "Observation LOINC 8867-4 Heart rate = 126 /min"); the `device` field on every FHIR Observation | Vital signs arrive from monitors and wearable patches as coded observations. They are never typed in by hand, which feeds the NEWS2 trend. SDC is the vendor-neutral standard for networked point-of-care devices. |
| **LOINC** | Observation codes | `00-core.js LOINC`; record vitals table; FHIR Observations; monitor and lab feeds | **Vital signs (5):** `8867-4` heart rate · `9279-1` respiratory rate · `59408-5` SpO₂ (pulse oximetry) · `8480-6` systolic BP · `8310-5` body temperature. **Labs (6, `10-engine.js LAB_TESTS`):** `718-7` haemoglobin · `2524-7` lactate · `10839-9` troponin I · `20563-3` carboxyhaemoglobin · `6690-2` leukocytes · `2345-7` glucose. Units use UCUM (`/min`, `mm[Hg]`, `%`, `Cel`). |
| **ICD-10-CM** | Diagnosis coding | Every patient row, record, FHIR Condition and ADT message (`00-core.js COND`) | 22 presenting conditions use 21 distinct codes (listed below). Codes for acute injuries carry the 7th character `A` for the initial encounter. UAE regulators and payers use ICD-10-CM for clinical coding (verify which edition is mandated). |
| **SNOMED CT** | Clinical terminology | Mapping target only (Standards modal) | **Not implemented in code.** In the design, the gateway maps ICD-10-CM to SNOMED CT for problem-list exchange with the national records. The prototype contains no SNOMED codes. |
| WHO **ATC** | Drug classification | `15-wasel.js DRUGS` (9 drugs); MedicationRequest feed | Each drug carries its ATC code, e.g. `J01CA04` amoxicillin, `B01AB01` heparin, `M01AE01` ibuprofen. Allergy checks match on drug class (penicillin, NSAID, contrast). |

**ICD-10-CM codes used (`00-core.js COND`)**
- **Road collision / trauma:** `T07.XXXA` multiple injuries · `S27.0XXA` traumatic pneumothorax · `S36.90XA` intra-abdominal injury · `S72.90XA` femur fracture · `S09.90XA` head injury · `S22.49XA` multiple rib fractures · `S52.509A` distal radius fracture · `S13.4XXA` cervical sprain · `S01.90XA` open wound of head
- **Building fire:** `T59.811A` smoke inhalation (used for mild and severe) · `T31.0` burn <10% TBSA · `T31.10` burn 10–19% · `T31.30` burn 30–39%
- **Everyday ED:** `R07.9` chest pain · `R10.9` abdominal pain · `R06.00` dyspnoea · `J45.901` asthma exacerbation · `A41.9` sepsis · `I63.9` ischaemic stroke · `R50.9` fever · `S93.409A` ankle sprain

### 2b. Clinical triage and scoring

| Standard | Area | Where in WASEL | How it complies (justification) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ESI v4** (AHRQ / ENA) | ED triage, 5 levels | ESI badge on every patient and bed; `ZONE_PREF` (bed zone); `W_ESI` (nurse workload); re-triage cards (`20-ai.js`) | ESI is the validated 5-level ED triage scale. WASEL displays and acts on the triage nurse's ESI level: ESI 1 goes to Resus first and ESI 4–5 to Fast track. Workload is weighted by ESI. When NEWS2 worsens, the AI only *suggests* a re-triage, and a physician or charge nurse approves it. The re-triage thresholds (NEWS2 ≥ 9 or SBP ≤ 85 → ESI 1, otherwise ESI 2) are a local rule, not part of ESI. |
| **START** | MCI field triage | Inbound cards in MCI mode (RED / YELLOW / GREEN chip); pre-alert intake (`15-wasel.js PREALERT_RULES`, `wslStart`) | EMS uses START colours in the field and the ED uses ESI, and WASEL accepts both. The crosswalk is: red → ESI 1, yellow → ESI 3, green → ESI 4, black → ESI 5. In the other direction, field patients get ESI ≤ 2 → red, ESI 3 → yellow, ESI 4–5 → green. Each colour is shown as text plus colour. The crosswalk is a local convention. |
| **NEWS2** (RCP, 2017) | Early warning score | Every patient row, drawer breakdown (`00-core.js news2 / news2Parts`); deterioration flags (`20-ai.js assess`) | All 7 parameters plus supplemental oxygen use the exact RCP bands (SpO₂ scale 1). An aggregate of 5–6 shows as *serious* (review within 30 min), and 7 or more as *critical* (urgent review). A single parameter scoring 3 is called out. A 12-minute trend detects deterioration in waiting patients, e.g. "HR 94→126, SBP 118→92". Scale 2 (hypercapnic patients) is not implemented. |
| ATMIST · SBAR · I-PASS | Structured handover | Pre-alert card and drawer (`wslAtmist`); SBAR block (`sbar()`); nurse handovers (`10-engine.js`, roster feed) | ATMIST covers the ambulance-to-ED pre-alert, SBAR the clinician handover, and I-PASS the nurse shift handover and reassignment. Using fixed formats reduces communication breakdowns, one of the three pain points in the brief. The SBAR block uses encounter IDs, not names. |

### 2c. Design and accessibility

| Standard | Area | Where in WASEL | How it complies (justification) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ISO 9241-210:2019** | Human-centred design | Whole UI: role-specific views, one decision queue, single-screen layout | The design is built around the context of use: who looks at the screen (charge nurse, physician, nurse, wall display, IT) and what each role needs during a surge. The AI watches, counts and matches, and humans decide. Every suggestion shows *why*, an owner and a clock. Critical data is never hidden behind tabs. |
| **WCAG 2.2 AA** | Accessibility | `shell.html`, `30-ui.js` | Controls are real `<button>` elements that work from the keyboard (Enter/Space, Escape closes dialogs) and return focus after a dialog closes. Critical decisions are announced through an `aria-live` region. Status is never shown by colour alone (SC 1.4.1): every status carries text and a glyph (▲ ●, mode shapes ● ◆ ▲ ■). Light and dark themes are provided and `<html lang>` is set. The Arabic RTL toggle is in progress †. |

### 2e. UAE law and health-information exchange

| Standard | Area | Where in WASEL | How it complies (justification) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UAE Federal Law No. 2 of 2019** (ICT in Health Fields) | Confidentiality, data localisation, emergency disclosure | CSP and in-memory state (`shell.html`); audit log; break-glass †; production hosting | **Confidentiality:** only roles with a clinical need see names or charts, and every access is logged. **Data localisation:** in the prototype all data stays in browser memory, and the CSP blocks every network connection. In production, servers and backups are hosted in the UAE. **Emergency disclosure:** urgent access outside the care team uses break-glass, which needs a written reason and is audited for later review. Article numbers: verify against official text. |
| **UAE PDPL**, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 | Biometrics as sensitive data; purpose limitation; minimisation | Pre-alert parser (`15-wasel.js wslParseIdentity`); `FACE_ID_INTEGRATION.md` rules; family board | Biometric data counts as *sensitive personal data*, so a face match is used for one purpose only: proposing a *candidate* identity at the door. No image, template, name or Emirates ID number is ever accepted. A `display` value that is not initials is discarded and logged. Biometrics are optional, and an opt-out shows as "Restricted by patient". The PDPL excludes health data that has its own legislation (Law No. 2 of 2019 is primary), so WASEL applies the PDPL principles as a baseline for biometrics. Verify the scope article and the status of the executive regulations. |
| **NABIDH** (DHA) · **Malaffi** (DoH Abu Dhabi) · **Riayati** (MOHAP / EHS) | Health-information-exchange policies and patient consent | Safety summary and history (`15-wasel.js wslLinkRecord`); identity confirmation feed | Each allergy, anticoagulant, blood-type and history line shows its **source exchange and as-of date** (provenance). When a patient has opted out (3% in the simulation), the record is **never loaded**. It shows as **"Restricted by patient"** and is treated as *Unknown*, never as "no allergies". A chart links through the exchange only after a human confirms the identity. |

### 2f. Emergency management and patient safety

| Standard | Area | Where in WASEL | How it complies (justification) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **JCI** Accreditation Standards for Hospitals (current edition) | Emergency management; patient safety goals | Mode ladder, surge, outage (`15-wasel.js updateMode`); identity flow; handovers | **Emergency/disaster management (FMS chapter):** the published Steady → Strain → Surge → MCI ladder, the silent-surge pack (recall 2 nurses, open 2 recovery beds, blood bank on standby) and downtime handling of the bed feed. **IPSG.1 (identify patients correctly):** a face match is one *candidate* identifier and never enough on its own. The patient stays on temporary ID `T-###` until a nurse confirms identity with a second source (the patient, a relative or documents). **IPSG.2 (effective communication):** ATMIST, SBAR and I-PASS. Verify the standard numbers for the current edition. |
| **NCEMA** (National Emergency Crisis and Disaster Management Authority) | National crisis and continuity framework | Mode changes posted to the feed tagged `NCEMA` and audited; Declare MCI †; family reunification board † | Readable escalation rules (`MODE_RULES`): **Strain** when occupancy ≥ 80%, an ESI 1–2 patient waits > 15 min or boarding exceeds 60 min. **Surge** when 2 or more strain rules hold, O-neg ≤ 10 units or ≤ 2 free ventilators. **MCI** when an incident brings ≥ 10 expected casualties or MCI is declared. The mode steps down only after 10 min, so it never flickers. Every change is logged with its reason. The family board shows status only, with no medical details. Verify the NCEMA standard edition (e.g. NCEMA 7000, business continuity) before citing it. |

## 3. Security and privacy controls

### 3a. Implemented in the prototype

| Control | What the code does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Role-based access (5 roles)** | **Wall display:** initials only, no records, no actions. **Charge nurse:** initial + surname, approves 15 task types. **Physician:** full names, clinical approvals. **Nurse (A. Haddad):** initial + surname, opens charts of assigned patients only, 4 task types †. **IT/Admin:** reads the audit log, has no chart rights. Each AI task type can be approved only by the roles listed for it, and blocked attempts are audited. | `00-core.js ROLES`, `canAct`; `30-ui.js handleAction` |
| **Wall-display de-identification** | Names become initials (`F.K.`) and MRNs are masked (`•••4821`) outside clinical roles. Opening a record is blocked and audited, and decision buttons are hidden. Pre-alert cards on the wall show the encounter ID, priority and ETA only †. | `30-ui.js pname`, `pmrn`, `openPatient` |
| **Break-glass †** | A nurse who opens a chart outside their care team sees a warning and must give a reason of at least 10 characters. The access is audited as `BREAK-GLASS: <enc> · reason: …`. The wall display and IT get no break-glass. The same reason rule is already enforced for medication hard-stop overrides (≥ 10 chars, control characters stripped, ≤ 200 chars, audited). | spec-v2 §UI 5; `15-wasel.js overrideOrder`, `WASEL_CFG.maxReasonLen` |
| **Audit log** | Records who (role) did what and when for: session start, role switches, record views, blocked attempts, accepted / dismissed / auto-accepted suggestions, escalations, mode changes, pre-alerts, identity confirmations, medication orders and overrides, and views of the audit log itself. Only IT/Admin can read it. | `00-core.js audit()`; `30-ui.js` feed/audit tab |
| **Auto-lock** | After 5 minutes with no pointer, keyboard, wheel or touch input, a clinical session drops to the wall display, closes any open chart and writes an audit entry. | `30-ui.js checkAutoLock` (`UI_LOCK_MS`) |
| **Escaped rendering** | All markup is built with an `html` tagged template that HTML-escapes every value through `esc()`, and `setHTML()` refuses raw strings. There is no `eval`, no inline event handler, and no third-party script, font or CDN. A CSP meta tag sets `default-src 'none'`, `base-uri 'none'` and `form-action 'none'`, so the page can make no network connections. The referrer policy is `no-referrer`. | `30-ui.js` header; `shell.html` CSP |
| **No PHI in storage or URLs** | All state lives in memory and is gone after a reload. The only `localStorage` key is the theme (`ercc.theme`). Nothing is passed in query strings or URL fragments. | `30-ui.js toggleTheme` |
| **Validation of untrusted input** | A pre-alert must have a valid envelope or it is rejected: `type`, `v: 1`, `encounter` matching `T-\d{1,6}`, a START or ESI priority, and `etaMin` of 0–240. Vital signs are range-checked (HR 20–250, SBP 40–260, RR 4–70, SpO₂ 40–100, GCS 3–15). Text is capped at 160 characters with control characters stripped. Undocumented fields are ignored. An invalid clinical field becomes *Unknown* and is logged, so a bad field never delays care. Sending the same encounter again updates it and never duplicates it. | `15-wasel.js wslParsePreAlert` and helpers |
| **Biometric candidate-only rules** | A face match only sets `status: candidate` plus its confidence. Confirmation is a separate human task (Receiving nurse, escalating to Charge nurse), and a candidate never merges charts. If no match is found, care continues on temporary ID `T-###`. Families see initials only after confirmation. Allergies `null` (Unknown) and `[]` (none) are kept distinct. | `15-wasel.js wslParseIdentity`, `wslInitials`, identity suggestion |
| **Medication safety** | Orders hard-stop on an allergy-class match or on heparin for an anticoagulated patient. Unknown allergy status gives an amber warning, never a silent pass. An override needs a reason, is audited and is sent as a MedicationRequest. | `15-wasel.js checkOrder`, `overrideOrder` |
| **Human in the loop and escalation** | Every suggestion has an owner and a clock (3 / 6 / 10 min by level) and escalates up the chain (Receiving nurse → Charge nurse → ED coordinator), with each escalation logged. Auto-accept is opt-in, limited to bed placements, changeable only by approvers, and logged as policy. | `15-wasel.js` tasks; `20-ai.js accept` |
| **Fail-safe data** | When a feed goes offline its source chip shows it. During a bed-system outage the last snapshot is overlaid as **STALE** and never shown as current, until the team switches to the backup RTLS bed sensors. | `30-ui.js renderBeds`; `10-engine.js SCEN.outage` |
| **Face-service proxy hardening** | `serve.py` binds to `127.0.0.1` only. It forwards only an allow-list of endpoints (`/health`, `/identify`, `/enroll`, `/people`) and returns 404 for anything else. Bodies over 15 MB get 413. Upstream calls time out after 60 s. An unreachable upstream returns a generic 502 with no internal detail. Other POSTs get 405. Every response carries `Cache-Control: no-store`, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` and `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN`. Request bodies (face photos) are never logged. | `serve.py` |

### 3b. Production design (not in the prototype)

| Area | Design |
|---|---|
| Identity and sign-in | SSO (OIDC / SAML) with **MFA** replaces the demo role selector, and roles come from directory groups. |
| Transport | **TLS 1.2+** (1.3 preferred) on every interface, with mutual TLS between the responder app, face service and gateway. |
| Data at rest | **AES-256** encryption at rest for the database, backups and the audit store. |
| Hosting | **UAE-hosted** servers and backups (Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 data localisation). |
| Message integrity | **Signed pre-alerts** (e.g. JWS) with timestamp and nonce for replay protection, and the gateway rejects unsigned messages. |
| Audit | Append-only, tamper-evident log streamed to a **SIEM**, with retention set by law. A privacy officer reviews every break-glass event. |
| Biometrics | Explicit consent registry. Templates are encrypted and stay on the device or in the UAE. `/enroll` and `/people` require authentication. |

## 4. Verify before presenting

1. **Features marked †** (break-glass, the Nurse role in the role selector, the BroadcastChannel pre-alert listener, Family desk, the medication-order UI, Declare MCI, the Arabic toggle, wall-display pre-alert cards) have engine support. When reviewed (24 Oct, 19:30), `30-ui.js` and `shell.html` had **not yet wired them**. In particular, `openPatient` only checks whether `detail` is truthy, so the nurse role (`detail: 'assigned'`) would open any chart *without* break-glass. Confirm all of these in the final build and remove the † marks.
2. **Auto-lock** covers only `charge` and `physician`. Add `nurse` to `checkAutoLock`.
3. **The in-app Standards & security modal** (`UI_STANDARDS`, `UI_CONTROLS`) still says "four roles" and is missing START, PDPL, the exchanges, JCI and NCEMA. Sync it with this file so judges see the same story on screen.
4. **ICD-10-CM spot checks:** confirm that `T07.XXXA` (7th character added in a recent code-set year) is valid in the edition you cite. The demo pre-alert says "open left femur fracture", but `S72.90XA` codes a *closed* fracture; an open one takes 7th character `B` (e.g. `S72.92XB`, verify). The "~35% full-thickness" major burn fits `T31.33` better than `T31.30`, and T31 extent codes are normally paired with a site code (T20–T25). The severe smoke case could add an airway-burn code. Also confirm which ICD-10-CM edition DoH and DHA mandate.
5. **Article numbers:** for Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 (data localisation, retention period, permitted disclosures) and the PDPL (biometric definition, health-data scope exclusion, executive regulations), verify against the official text before quoting any article. Don't cite numbers you can't show.
6. **JCI / NCEMA / ADHICS references:** verify the JCI edition and standard IDs (IPSG.1, IPSG.2, the FMS emergency-management standard), the NCEMA standard name and edition, and the ADHICS version. If asked about Dubai or the northern emirates, DHA (NABIDH) and EHS / MOHAP (Riayati) have their own policies.
7. **Exchange consent models:** check how NABIDH, Malaffi and Riayati each word and handle patient opt-out, and that "Restricted by patient" is acceptable wording.
8. **FHIR simplifications (say so if asked):** Bundle `fullUrl`s use `urn:uuid:` with non-UUID IDs, which a strict validator would flag. BP is a standalone `8480-6` rather than the FHIR vital-signs BP panel (`85354-9` with components). The R4 vital-signs SpO₂ profile expects `2708-6` alongside `59408-5`. Location, SupplyRequest, MedicationRequest and ServiceRequest appear as feed messages, not full resources. SNOMED CT is design only.
9. **Clinical scope:** NEWS2 is validated for adults; it is not for patients under 16 or in pregnancy, and scale 2 is not implemented. The START black → ESI 5 mapping is a local routing choice (no bed is reserved, but the patient stays visible), so be ready to justify it.
10. **Face service:** `FACE_ID_INTEGRATION.md` says no templates are stored, yet the proxy exposes `/enroll` and `/people`. Confirm what the face service stores and that `/people` never returns names to the dashboard. The proxy has no authentication (localhost demo only).
11. **Audit log** is in memory and keeps only the last 150 entries. Present it as a demo, with SIEM in production.
12. **HIPAA / NIST** are comparison frameworks, not UAE obligations. **All data is synthetic.** Disclose AI-assisted code if the organisers ask.
